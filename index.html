How to use MedTourney

What is this?

MedTourney is a search tool for European chess tournaments, with a focus on Mediterranean seaside locations and senior-friendly events. Data is fetched daily from chess-results.com.

Getting started

Results for the next 6 months load automatically — results update as soon as you change a filter. Click anywhere on a card to open its page on chess-results.com. Your filter settings are saved automatically.

Key filters

  • Open CategoryShows only tournaments open to all skill levels — not invitation-only or club-only events.
  • Exclude YouthHides tournaments aimed at players under 18.
  • SeasideTournaments within about 10 km of the Mediterranean coast (incl. the Adriatic and Aegean) or the Atlantic coasts of Spain and Portugal, plus well-known coastal towns. A 🏖 Beachfront badge marks venues found to be within 500 m of the sea.
  • S50+ / S60+Senior age categories. Checking both shows all senior events (OR logic).
  • Rating ceilingU1400–U2200 — shows only club-level tournaments below a rating limit.
  • Youth age groupTargets a specific under-age group (overrides the exclude-youth filter).
  • Time ControlClassical, Rapid, or Blitz. All three checked = show everything.
  • DurationWeekend-only to multi-week vacations — useful for travel planning.
  • CountryTick one or more countries for OR filtering. In Seaside mode, only countries with seaside tournaments are shown.

Featured card

When a seaside tournament you could plan a trip around starts within 30 days (an Open event of 5–16 days, not a club league, team or youth event), it appears highlighted in amber above the results — independently of your active filters. Beachfront venues are preferred.

Shortlist & export

Click ★ on any card to save it. Use Export Shortlist to download all starred tournaments as a single .ics file for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Hover or focus a card to reveal Add to Calendar for a single-tournament export, and Copy link to share it directly.
