How to use MedTourney

What is this?

MedTourney is a search tool for European chess tournaments, with a focus on Mediterranean seaside locations and senior-friendly events. Data is fetched daily from chess-results.com.

Getting started

Set your filters, then click Search. Click any tournament name to open its page on chess-results.com. Your filter settings are saved automatically.

Key filters

  • Open CategoryShows only tournaments open to all skill levels — not invitation-only or club-only events.
  • Exclude YouthHides tournaments aimed at players under 18.
  • MediterraneanLimits results to coastal Mediterranean cities: Barcelona, Nice, Athens, Malta, Split, and more.
  • S50+ / S60+Senior age categories. Checking both shows all senior events (OR logic).
  • Rating ceilingU1400–U2200 — shows only club-level tournaments below a rating limit.
  • Youth age groupTargets a specific under-age group (overrides the exclude-youth filter).
  • Time ControlClassical, Rapid, or Blitz. All three checked = show everything.
  • DurationWeekend-only to multi-week vacations — useful for travel planning.
  • CountryTick one or more countries for OR filtering. When Mediterranean is active, only Med-compatible countries are shown.

Featured card

When a qualifying Mediterranean tournament starts within 30 days and lasts more than 5 days, it appears highlighted in amber above the results — independently of your active filters.

Shortlist & export

Click ★ on any card to save it. Use Export Shortlist to download all starred tournaments as a single .ics file for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Each card also has 📅 Add to Calendar for a single-tournament export.
